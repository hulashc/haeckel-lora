Root cause, found and fixed

The background-color bias

Captions never mentioned background color, so the model guessed. Fixed by measuring each plate's real background from its pixels — a clean, wide split, not a guess.

52 black
48 pale

Two rounds of fine-tuning later (including one that doubled pale-example exposure for 40 more epochs), here's the honest result on the 12-plate eval sample — no net gain between rounds, some plates just traded places:
